GAME DOCTOR 2.0

Game Doctor checks games for mistakes and explains, in plain words, what is
wrong, what players will notice and how to fix it.

Works with Twine games (SugarCube, Harlowe, Chapbook, Snowman), other HTML
games, Ren'Py, RPG Maker MV/MZ, Ink and ChoiceScript.

Safe to use: Game Doctor only reads your game. It never changes your game
files. Every fix is a suggestion; you decide what to change.

Optional extras:
- Play-test needs Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome (already on most PCs).
- The AI helper needs an API key from an AI service, or LM Studio / Ollama
  on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you use it.

Your settings are kept in %APPDATA%\GameDoctor and are not removed when you
uninstall.
